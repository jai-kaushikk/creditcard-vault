Your cards stay on your phone.

CardVault is a card wallet for friends who lend each other their credit cards. Share a card, approve every payment with your OTP, and keep a fair ledger of who owes what.

Download for Android Source code

Encrypted on your phone

Card numbers are stored in your phone’s secure keystore and only shown after a fingerprint or face check.

You approve every payment

A friend paying with your card asks you for the OTP. You decide, every time.

Recorded only on success

When the payment goes through it lands in both ledgers: lent for you, owed for them.

We can’t read your cards

Shares and OTPs are end-to-end encrypted. Our server stores transactions, never card details.
